# Secure Delivery Intake

## Status

Idea / Not currently in scope

## Purpose

A concept for handling deliveries entering a high-security facility.

## Problem

How can deliveries be received, verified, tracked, and transferred
without weakening zone security or chain of custody?

## Potential relationships

- Identity and credentials
- Protected zones
- Watcher operations
- Incident workflow
- Audit and traceability

## Open questions

- Who owns the intake process?
- Is there a dedicated receiving zone?
- How are unexpected deliveries handled?
- What happens when a delivery cannot be verified?

## Scope note

This is a future idea only. It is not part of the current operational model.
